## Hook (Findlay's speech bubble, transcribed)
"It's me, Findlay, owner of the Lamp Emporium on Artisan Avenue. I need your help! Yesterday I spilled tea on my demonic contracts. This released the imp workforce who make my lamps and also freed my devil servant, Belvedere, who has taken over my shop for himself. I was lucky to escape alive! One imp bit off my finger, and they have set up a magic ward that stops me from returning. Belvedere threatened to burn my spell book if I try anything. So please be sneaky! Belvedere's horn is hidden in a secret compartment in my commode in the master bedroom. If he gets hold of it he'll be restored to his full dangerous power. Here's the key to the front door."
Findlay is drawn as a frantic orange-haired man in a bow tie, one hand bandaged/missing a finger.

## Rewards ("I will reward you handsomely")
- 4 gp: per imp tail returned to me
- 25 gp: retrieve my severed finger
- 40 gp: send Belvedere back through the portal
- 80 gp: destroy the ward keeping me from my home
- 100 gp: retrieve my spell book
- 120 gp: retrieve the blueprints for making lamps

## Initial Imp Reaction (d6; "if you can't determine from the hero's actions and from the imp pictures")
1 Friendly; 2 Curious; 3 Shy; 4 Afraid; 5 Angry; 6 Murderous

2. Sleep draught. 6 hours.
3. Removes your anxiety but also your motivation.
4. Aura sight. Very trippy.
5. Instant laxative. Very potent.

## Areas (floor by floor)
### 1. Showroom Floor (imps present: Bif, Poo, Fog)
- 1A: Front door. Sign taped on: "CLOSED for business. Opening soon under new management. -B" (a "Welcome" mat outside).
- 1B: Bif, Poo & Fog are painting a new sign for "Belvedere's Lamp Emporium".
- 1C: 14 silver pieces in the cash register.
- 1D: 5 Impish Bonbons and a bottle of gin in the very securely locked lockbox.
- 1E: Butler's quarters. But Belvedere has moved upstairs to the master bedroom. Potion in the nightstand. 1 bonbon under the pillow.
- Sidebar "Impish Bonbons": special candy that imps love more than anything else.

- 2D: Balefire font. Magical, infinite supply.

## Creatures (no AC/HP printed; only abilities)
- BELVEDERE (red devil butler, bow tie, vest, one horn): can hypnotise one person at a time. Can turn to smoke & escape. If other horn is attached he doubles in strength and size. If both horns removed then shrinks to size of an imp. Carries tin of bonbons & motivating whip (if hit, pain disables for 1d3 rounds).
- MAX: leader of revolutionaries (brown bat-winged imp with a wand). Wants to overthrow Belvedere. Can turn into a bat. Looking for Belvedere's other horn. Carries Wand of Shrinkray, 4 charges.
- BIF (green, orc-like with club): dumb. Strong as an orc. Loyal to Max.
- POO (dark grey, cat/skunk-like): cheeky. Sprays frictionless grease from rear gland.
- YUK (purple, spider-like): mute. Paralysing venom on claws. Paralysis for 1d6 rounds.
- FAY (pale blue/teal, big ears): can create illusions. Loyal to Max. Balefire Molotov.
- PUP (pink, baby-like): so cute. Loves to murder. Will save to attack him (text: "will save to attack him" - exact meaning unclear).
- LEO (yellow leopard-spotted): giggles a lot. Regenerates all damage instantly.
- HUH (shadowy/black smoke figure with knife): loves to kill. Smells of ammonia. Turns invisible. Loyal to Belvedere. Extra sharp glass knife.
- FOG (grey, belching smoke): belches dark smoke. Loyal to Belvedere.
- ZAP (blue, round): sings. Will do anything for Impish Bonbons. Casts magic missile. Loyal to Max.
- Factions: Max's revolutionaries (Bif, Fay, Zap, Yuk?, Leo?, Pup? - only Bif, Fay, Zap, Max explicitly "loyal to Max") vs Belvedere's loyalists (Huh, Fog explicitly "loyal to Belvedere"). Others unstated.
- Imp tails are the 4 gp per-tail bounty.

## Balefire (sidebar)
- Doesn't radiate heat but does burn like fire on contact.
- Slowly evaporates if not contained.
- Burning does not spread but flames can be transferred to a more flammable object.
- Smells minty.

## Objective / ending
Infiltrate the tower quietly, suppress the imp revolution, recover Findlay's assets (finger, spell book, blueprints), destroy the ward, send Belvedere back through the portal (4A), prevent Belvedere reuniting with his second horn (4D). Rewards listed above.
